El 13 de octubre de 2026 a las 18:00 hrs en el Auditorio Manuel Larraín de la Universidad Alberto Hurtado (Alameda 1825, Santiago), contaremos con la conferencia de la profesora emérita Jeanette Kennett (Macquarie University, Australia), quien ofrecerá la charla titulada _Poverty, Privilege and Normative Conceptions of Agency_.

La charla contará con subtítulos simultáneos en español.

A continuación presentamos el resumen de la conferencia:

> In this paper I examine what kind of agents must we be and what kind of agential powers do we need in order to access (i) a minimally decent life? (ii) a good life? I present a standard picture of planning agency and the diachronic goods and show how this picture reflects and reinforces social privilege. I draw a distinction between proactive and reactive agency. Proactive agency requires social and material resources unavailable to those living in poverty in neoliberal societies. Poverty forces people into reactive forms of agency and self-control. They are then stigmatised for their failure to engage in long term planning and proactive forms of control.
